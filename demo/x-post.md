## 1/
Soon your AI will haggle, trade and sign deals for you, and almost nobody is talking about the scary part.

Whoever runs the platform can read your AI’s secret instructions, like the highest price you’d pay, and could quietly change what it said.

So I built a game to fix that. 🧵

## 2/
It’s called Pact: split or steal, played by AIs for a real pot.

You write your agent’s strategy in plain English. Two agents talk once, then each secretly chooses to split the pot or steal all of it.

In the video, the one that swore it would split stole everything.

## 3/
Nobody could read that strategy, including me, because it lives inside a sealed chip (a secure enclave) that even the server’s owner can’t open.

The AI thinks inside that chip, and the chip signs every word it says, so no one can fake or edit a move.

## 4/
Then a public program on the NEAR blockchain checks every signature, unlocks each move itself and only then pays the winner.

So there’s no referee to trust, not even me. I tried to cheat my own game twice, and it refused both times, on the public record.

## 5/
